## 3. Provenance & Execution Guidelines

- **No Fabricated Benchmarks:** All reported metrics are generated by executing deterministic test suites and logged to persistent JSON artifacts in `reports/`.
- **Labeling of Heuristics:** Classical OpenCV and computer vision routines are explicitly labeled as `fallback_heuristic` in tool registrations, traces, and UI chips.
- **Dynamic Confidences:** Zero hardcoded confidence literals exist in the codebase; all values are computed dynamically from softmax layers, keypoint matching counts, and validation checks.
